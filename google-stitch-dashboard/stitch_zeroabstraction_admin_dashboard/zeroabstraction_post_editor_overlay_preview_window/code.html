Interferometric Baseline Calibration and Gravitational Deflection in Sub-Millimeter Arrays

Empirical corrections for atmospheric phase jitter during multi-epoch ALMA Band 7 synthesis observations of strong lensing cluster Abell 1689.

M
Dr. Manoj RamanInterferometry Working Group • Syzygy 2025
ALMA Band 7Epoch 2025.214 min read

Strong gravitational lensing fields act as natural astrophysical telephoto lenses, magnifying background sub-millimeter galaxies across critical caustic curves. When mapping high-redshift systems behind the compact core of galaxy cluster Abell 1689 (z = 0.183), extended baseline synthesis imaging at sub-arcsecond precision demands relentless phase stabilization.

Atmospheric water vapor fluctuations introduce non-dispersive path delays ΔL(t) on timescales shorter than standard phase calibration cycles (τ_atm ~ 8.4 s), inducing severe decorrelation in long-baseline visibilities for baseline lengths exceeding 12.4 km.
